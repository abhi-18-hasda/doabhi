🛡️ Why Did Your Previous Link Get Suspended? (And Permanent Fix)

When you drag-and-drop a file onto Netlify Drop without logging in, Netlify creates a temporary demo link that automatically expires after 1 to 24 hours.

  • Permanent Fix A (Netlify Free Account): Sign in with a free email/GitHub account on app.netlify.com/drop before dragging your index.html folder. Your DoAbhi app will stay live forever and never suspend!
  • Permanent Fix B (tiiny.host or GitHub Pages): Upload index.html to GitHub Pages or Vercel for a permanent URL.
